Experiment story

What changed as the system matured?

1Start simple

A hierarchical median baseline established whether ML was actually adding value.

2Fix representation

Model-family normalization produced a much larger gain than simply adding more numeric features.

3Evaluate retrieval

Hit@k, MRR and nDCG were added so ranking quality was not hidden behind one demo.

4Design for failure

When the generator missed the citation instruction, the final architecture added validation and a safe fallback.

Data & evaluation

Why the numbers are not presented as “accuracy”.

Price prediction is regression. Review search is information retrieval. They need different evaluation language.

Price modelChronological holdout • MAE • RMSE • MAPE • R² • tolerance bands. Asking price is the target, not final sale price.
Review retrievalHit@1/3/5 • MRR@10 • nDCG@10 • latency. Identity retrieval is treated as a sanity metric, not universal semantic relevance.
External-comparison rule: public Craigslist and RAG projects are used to motivate experiments and evaluation design, not to claim a misleading leaderboard win across different splits, features or relevance labels.
