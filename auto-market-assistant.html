The system can abstain.

Questions first pass a capability guard that blocks exact-vehicle condition/history, current transactional data and authoritative-specification requests. In-scope questions then pass a calibrated evidence gate using max similarity, mean top-3 similarity and the number of supporting reviews. Generated source IDs are validated against the reviews that were actually retrieved.

Experiment story

What changed as the system matured?

1Start simple

A hierarchical median baseline established whether ML was actually adding value.

2Fix representation

Model-family normalization produced a much larger gain than simply adding more numeric features.

3Evaluate retrieval

Hit@k, MRR and latency were measured so retrieval quality was not hidden behind one demo.

4Calibrate abstention

A held-out weakly supervised benchmark separated source capability from retrieval relevance and selected a multi-signal operating point instead of a magic cosine cutoff.

Data & evaluation

Why the numbers are not presented as “accuracy”.

Price prediction is regression. Review search is information retrieval. They need different evaluation language.

Price modelChronological holdout • MAE • RMSE • MAPE • R² • tolerance bands. Asking price is the target, not final sale price.
Review retrievalHit@1/3/5 • MRR@10 • latency • held-out answerability precision/recall/F1. Identity retrieval and weak-label answerability are reported separately rather than collapsed into one “RAG accuracy” number.
External-comparison rule: public Craigslist and RAG projects are used to motivate experiments and evaluation design, not to claim a misleading leaderboard win across different splits, features or relevance labels.
Future work

What I would test next.

The production-oriented pipeline is intentionally frozen at a validated operating point. The next improvements are research extensions, not required fixes.

Human-labelled relevance setManually grade question-review pairs and use that gold set to compare dense retrieval with cross-encoder reranking.
Stronger retrieval representationsTest passage-level chunking and newer embedding families such as BGE or E5 on the exact same held-out labels.
Faithfulness evaluationAdd claim-level entailment checks so citation validity is complemented by evidence-to-claim support measurement.
Production generationEvaluate whether a managed or larger generator improves synthesis enough to justify additional latency and infrastructure cost.
Current answerability result: 80.95% accuracy, 90.0% precision, 84.38% recall and 87.10% F1 on a held-out weakly supervised benchmark across unseen vehicle families. These are not presented as universal RAG accuracy.
